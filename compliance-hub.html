A robust integration feature that empowers Sellers to effectively manage their inventory while also meeting state compliance requirements & regulations.

Overview

Company
LeafLink
Role
Product Design & Research
Time frame
15 months

Seed to sale compliance is a requirement for every cannabis business. Most states use Metrc, an application that tracks the lifecycle of every cannabis product, from seedling → harvest → process → retail. It is illegal to sell cannabis products without the compliance ID attached to it. Businesses find Metrc very frustrating to use, as it is unintuitive and prone to outages.

To address this industry pain point, we built a robust integration feature that allows our customers to manage their compliance data within our platform. This data sync enables our customers to effectively market their products, price inventory by potency, and streamline supply chain operations.

Adhering to state compliance requirements

Package data is stored and managed in Metrc, but it is a finicky application that most cannabis businesses hate dealing with. The user experience is poor, outages happen frequently, and the company does not address any of these pain points in a timely manner, if at all.

LeafLink has limited Metrc integration capabilities, like syncing inventory amounts, but the current state is still very manual and inefficient.

Metrc integration feature (Before)

Approach

The integration’s strongest feature is mapping Metrc data to our customers’ products in LeafLink. With this, they can map Metrc data to their LeafLink products, auto-generate invoices, price inventory by THC%, and provide crucial product information to Buyers. It is important for us to empower customers with the tools they need to run their business successfully.
